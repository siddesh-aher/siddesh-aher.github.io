About

What I actually do

At Morgan Stanley, I support investment performance measurement and reporting for institutional clients. I led the strategic migration of Separately Managed Accounts from BNY Mellon to Aladdin — requirements definition, data mapping, parallel testing, and production deployment — partnering with front office, technology, operations, and custodial teams to keep data integrity and performance accuracy intact through a controlled transition. I also support performance validation for SMAs on Aladdin and mutual funds on FactSet B-One: return calculations, cash-flow treatment, fee impacts, benchmark alignment, and exception analysis.

Day to day that means Python, SQL, and PySpark for data validation and transformation inside Snowflake, Tableau for the dashboards people actually look at, and Alteryx and Dataiku for the automated workflows in between — plus data governance and lineage tracking (Apache Atlas) to keep outputs audit-ready. Before Morgan Stanley, I built live BI dashboards at Genentech and optimized SQL performance at ASK Industries.

Right now I'm finishing an MS in Data Science at Northeastern University (GPA 3.8), and using the coursework as a chance to go deeper on both the data engineering side — AWS, Apache Spark, cloud ETL — and the modeling side — classification, clustering, text mining, and anomaly detection — which is what the projects below are drawn from.
